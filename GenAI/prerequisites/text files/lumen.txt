Logic Rule (LMF-01): Any fixture exceeding 800LMF must utilize MCPCB (Metal Core Printed Circuit Boards). Standard FR4 boards are strictly prohibited due to delamination risks. 
Ancillary Component (LMF-AC1): For 1000LMF to 1200LMF, add Thermal Interface Material (TIM-09) and Heavy-Duty Aluminum Extrusion (XT-70). 
The "CRI-Heat" Offset: If CRI > 90, increase thermal budget by 12%. High-CRI phosphors convert more energy to heat than standard 80CRI chips. NEW Logic Rule (LMF-02): If CCT = 50K and LMF > 1000, add High-Flow Heat Sink (HS-MAX) due to increased blue-diode thermal density. Ancillary Component (LMF-AC2): If LMF > 1000 AND Shape = RPP/QDP, add High-Flow End-Cap Vents (EC-VENT).
